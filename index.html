How I work

Based in Tunisia, I work with clients around the globe thanks to video calls and remote computer control. Throughout my support services, data protection and the privacy of my clients have the highest priority. No matter if you are a start-up, local NGO or an established company, my goal is to work with you on sustainable IT solutions that are comprehensive and can be handled by the client without extra support as far as possible.

About Me

I am an IT specialist who has worked at cultural institutions as well as international organisations. Apart from ensuring a smoothly working IT infrastructure for Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung and backend management for GIZ's online courses, I was part of the setup of the digital film archive of Tunisia's national film institute (Cinémathèque).
